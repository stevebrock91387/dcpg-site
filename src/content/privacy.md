# Privacy Policy

**Decoupage Screenwriting** is developed by Steve Brock. This policy explains what the app does — and, just as importantly, what it doesn't do — with your information. *Draft — under review.*

**Last updated:** July 22, 2026

## The short version

Decoupage Screenwriting is a **local-first** Mac app. Your scripts and production data live **on your device**. There is **no account**, **no sign-in**, and **no analytics or tracking of any kind**. The only time anything leaves your Mac is when *you* explicitly choose a cloud action, or send us a support message.

## What's stored, and where

Your screenplays and project data are saved as **document files on your Mac** (`.dcpg` packages) that you own and control. The app does not maintain a user account and does not sync your work to any server. Back up, move, and open these files like any other document.

## On-device AI is private

The app's on-device reads — craft checks, scene digests, and Read Aloud narration — run **entirely on your Mac using Apple's on-device models and locally bundled components**. For these features, **your script never leaves your device.**

## Cloud reads are opt-in

Some deeper reads can optionally run in the cloud. These are **never automatic**: the app clearly labels them, and your screenplay text is sent off your device **only when you explicitly choose a cloud read**, and only for that request. Depending on your choice and system, a cloud read is processed by:

- **Apple** (Private Cloud Compute), on supported systems; or
- **Anthropic** (Claude), when you opt into a Claude read.

Your text is used only to generate the read you asked for. See Apple's and Anthropic's privacy policies for how they handle data they process on your behalf.

## Cast and contact information

If you enter cast or crew contact details for production paperwork, the app follows **data-minimization**: it keeps only what a production needs (such as name, email, phone, and agent), uses **device-local identifiers**, and does **not** store sensitive identifiers like Social Security numbers, dates of birth, or home addresses. You control which fields appear on any document you export.

## Support messages

If you contact us through the support form on this site, your message and email address are delivered to the developer by our email provider (**Resend**) so we can reply. We use this information only to respond to you.

## No tracking, no analytics, no ads

The app contains **no analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-tracking SDKs**, and does not build a profile of you or your usage.

## Third-party services

- **Apple** — the App Store handles your purchase; Apple's terms and privacy policy apply. On supported systems, Apple's Private Cloud Compute may process an opt-in cloud read.
- **Anthropic** — processes a Claude cloud read only when you opt in.
- **Resend** — delivers support messages you send us.

## Children

Decoupage Screenwriting is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
